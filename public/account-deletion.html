Delete Your Account

Tactical Command · account & data deletion requests

If you signed in to Tactical Command for online play and want your account removed, email us from the Google account you used to sign in. No account is created for offline play — offline progress lives only on your device and is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Request deletion by email

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (Google display name and email link)
  • Your player profile, level, and cosmetic selections
  • Your match history, leaderboard entries, and chat messages

Deletion is permanent and covers all data we hold about your account. We aim to complete requests within 14 days and will confirm by reply once done.

Partial deletion

If you'd rather keep your account but remove specific data (for example, chat messages), say so in your email and we'll action that instead.
