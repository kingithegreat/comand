Privacy Policy

Tactical Command · Last updated: 11 July 2026

No adsNo analytics SDKsNo tracking

Tactical Command ("the game", "we") is a turn-based tactics game developed by an independent developer based in New Zealand. This policy explains what information the game handles and why. We collect the minimum needed to run the game's online features — nothing more.

What we collect

If you play offline modes only (VS AI, Pass & Play, Campaign, Tutorial): no personal information is collected or sent to us. Your progress, settings, and squad formations are stored locally on your device.

If you sign in for online play (optional), we collect via Google Firebase:

  • Account details — your Google account display name and email address, used to identify your account.
  • Player profile — the display name you choose, level, and cosmetic selections.
  • Gameplay records — match results, leaderboard standings, and in-match chat messages, stored so online play and rankings work.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or any data unrelated to the game.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with third parties for their own purposes.

How your data is stored

Online data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC, and is encrypted in transit. Google acts as our data processor; their handling is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

Data retention & deletion

Online account data is kept while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our account deletion page, or email us directly. We aim to action deletion requests within 14 days.

Children

The game is rated for everyone aged 10 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Online sign-in requires a Google account, which has its own age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 (and equivalent laws where you live, such as the GDPR), you may request access to or correction of your personal information, and request its deletion. Contact us using the details below.

Changes
